Naomi Review

112 Atlantic Ave, Toronto, ON · Liberty Village & Parkdale

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

The male Asian owner is racist against Blacks. He did not greet me on two different occasions. Just stared at me blankly when I walked in to his restaurant and waited for me to speak. The second time I said it's common courtesy to greet people when they walk into your establishment. So he did, got pissed at the situation then walked off to the kitchen area and left me standing there. The female making the food tried to chime in and correct things, but why would I spend my money at such a crappy lookng place with an a-hole owner? I went home and made my own fried egg on toast.
