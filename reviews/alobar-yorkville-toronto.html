Alobar Yorkville Review

162 Cumberland St, Toronto, ON · Yorkville & The Annex

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Our favorite menu items were: the scalloped ceviche, beef tartare (special), "Fruit Cup" cocktail, grilled tiger prawn, and the scallops with Jalapeno peppers. The (cherry) cheesecake was one of the best we've ever had! Did a review on it on IG @chowhoundgta.
