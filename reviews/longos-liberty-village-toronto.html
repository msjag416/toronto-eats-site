Longo's Liberty Village Review

1100 King St W, Toronto, ON

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Friendly, helpful staff. Clean store. Mike is available to bring most orders they don’t have in. I gave it 4/5 stars because they’re a smaller store and they usually are out of some of the things I like, so I have to online order or telephone order, and go back on another date. So I have to plan more and can’t be spontaneous. I usually end up going and getting the specific thing I’m waiting on at Loblaws or Metro. Also, the buffet was nice, but that's a thing of the past apparently. The bar to watch sports, have a beer or cocktail is in limbo. And finally, if you want a specific cut of beef, you are $hit out of luck, because no one will cut it for you.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
