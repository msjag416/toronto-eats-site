Cineplex Cinemas Varsity and VIP Review

55 Bloor St W, Toronto, ON · Rosedale, Cabbagetown & Summerhill

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Watched Argo at the silly 25 seat VIP theatre yesterday. Great movie in a stupid venue. The VIP theatre is small, and smelly. A complete waste of $20. Stay home and get Apple TV (jailbroken) a surround sound system, and have a good time. The extra $5 is not worth it to be in VIP.

Good: Roomy seats if you are overweight, and lots of leg space if you're tall. A side table to put your beer and stuff. $3.00 underground parking with validated ticket after 6pm.

Bad: Seats have rips, and are are in bad shape. Somebody's head will be in the way if you are right behind them and they are tall. The place smells old. The SCREEN is 1980's small. The SOUND is not the current industry standard.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
