The Originals Shore Club Miami Beach Review

1901 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL, United States

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Stayed at the Shore Club for 10 days from Sept - Oct 2016. It was my birthday and also my friend's birthday, and we told "Jordan" ahead of time. Unexpectedly, we were upgraded to an ocean view suite. The room had a very comfortable bed, balcony, and great big bathroom. I also received a birthday cheesecake for my birthday. We asked for a refrigerator to store our cold drinks and they gave us one. For the entire stay, we were treated great. The pool party on Sunday was the best with a live DJ playing great house music.
My only complaint is the lighting in the bathroom. It wasn't fluorescent, so you can't see what shade of makeup you end up wearing. As well a 10x mirror needs to be somewhere in there as well to pluck your brows.
