The Keg Steakhouse + Bar - King West Review

560 King St W, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

I've gone twice to this King Street West location in 2015 and I can say the food, service, and ambiance is consistently good. The lobster, king crab, and steaks are all winners, and so are all of the appetizers. The ice cream cake is great too. Make a reservation if you plan on going, but the best area to be in is the bar seating area. For some reason they can't guarantee reservations there however.
