Forest Hill Spa Review

52 St Clair Ave W, Toronto, ON · The Junction, High Park & Roncesvalles

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Not the best massage one can get. You pretty much pay for the environment here and not the quality of a good massage.

If you need a full body massage by someone who knows his muscle groups, has the patience and desire to learn what your muscles respond to, and is not just going through the motions of his job as a massage therapist...get a 90 min massage from Fabio. Fabio has the right techniques to knead out those knots, poke, and prod those trigger points, and will leave you feeling revitalized at the end of the session.
