Tryst Review

82 Peter St, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

What do you get when you put together 3 uniquely stylish rooms and a courtyard with the music of a well know Toronto DJ? Success, in spite of the fact that Tryst is still just getting off the ground. The newest venue to the club district caters to a selective 23+ clientele, and can accommodate 800 partiers.

Full review here: http://w4wtoronto.wordpress.com/2008/04/08/tryst-april-5th-2008
