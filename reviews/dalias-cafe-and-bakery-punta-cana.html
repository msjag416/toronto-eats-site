Dalia's Cafe & Bakery Review

Plaza Turquesa, Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

The drinks are watered down nonsense. Especially their 2 for 1 special. Dinner food is atrocious and looks better than it is. I don’t recommend this place if you have taste buds. This after a slick guy got us to go in and waste our money. Not fine dining or even the Keg standard.
