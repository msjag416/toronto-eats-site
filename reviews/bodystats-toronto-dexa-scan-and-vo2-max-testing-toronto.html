BodyStats Toronto: Dexa Scan and Vo2 Max Testing Review

100 Western Battery Rd Unit 2, Toronto, ON · Liberty Village & Parkdale

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Visited April 6th 2026.

BodyStats made getting a DEXA scan easy quick and stress-free.

We went in today for a scan with Paul the owner and the whole appointment took about 15 minutes. The place was clean organized and had a friendly welcoming vibe from the moment we walked in. It’s also reasonably priced which makes it even better.

A nice bonus is that you can bring a friend or partner with you and have your DEXA scans done one after the other, which makes the whole experience even nicer. Your results are emailed to you immediately after the scan so there’s no waiting around wondering. The emailed information and image was clear, and was exactly what we wanted.

Overall a very smooth, professional experience.
