Balti Indian Restaurant Review

2241a Bloor St W, Toronto, ON · The Junction, High Park & Roncesvalles

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We went to Bukhara Grill in Oct 2022 on a weekday. A Monday to be specific, during Diwali.

We were greeted with friendly service in a clean and intimate setting.

The food was fresh, flavorful, spicy, and tasted amazing. We had the Beef Madras, Chicken Tikka Masala, and the absolutely delightful "Goat Masaleder" with the bones in (goat meat cooked with tomato and onion gravy). Actually, authentic tasting Indian food.

We had 4 dishes for the two of us and took home leftovers. The leftovers tasted even better 10 hours later!

Oh, and you won't spend a fortune eating the upscale food here either, unlike in downtown Toronto -- where you'll have mediocre, inauthentic Indian food at marked up prices.
