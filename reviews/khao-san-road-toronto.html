Khao San Road Review

11 Charlotte St, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

The best, most delectable, and most authentic Thai food in Toronto! Only Sukho Thai comes in as a close second in taste and a better value. Be prepared to wait at Kao San road if you didn't make reservations, and if you didn't get there as soon as they opened for dinner.
