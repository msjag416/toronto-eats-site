Toronto General Hospital Review

200 Elizabeth St, Toronto, ON · Downtown Yonge & Dundas

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Best hospital to work in Intensive Care as a Registered Nurse. Just have a couple of years experience on the floors as a post graduate Nurse, then a couple of years experience as a Critical Care Nurse at a hospital. The ICU's at TGH are clean, and comfortable, modern, and equipped. Staff is friendly and accepting of you if you show you are quick, competent, and a team player.
