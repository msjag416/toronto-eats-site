Endy Review

Online business

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

We waited a year to do this Endy mattress review, because we never liked it in the begining, but gave it a chance.

We had an Endy queen mattress and just got rid of it after a year (over 300 sleeps). The foam in an Endy mattress degrades quickly and cannot support your back over time because they have no coil system inside. We have a closely slatted base foundation bed frame (from Structube) that supported the mattress and after a few months, bought and tried a topper which continued to make us feel hot. The Endy mattress is uncomfortably hot by itself. I weigh 135-140lbs and my partner weighs 150-155lbs. We have had back and neck pain for several months now and have finally given up on the Endy mattres! How much more instruction do I need on how to use a mattress?!

We went online recently to National Mattress and ordered a Serta Perfect Sleeper queen pillow top mattress and they delivered one day later for free. We used their $100 off coupon order and paid just over $1000 for the mattress. This is the same mattress used at the Royal Fairmont Hotel, and at Hilton hotels. It is 50% cheaper than ordering from the Fairmont online, and a couple of hundred bucks cheaper than getting it from the Brick. I chose this mattress because I slept like a baby on it numerous times over the years and never thought to ask the hotels what mattress it was. I didn't even think it was affordable.

We wasted our money getting an Endy mattress-- point blank. It is not made for adult bodies. And doing a review on it after 2 weeks or a month is not enough time to realize the grave mistake you've made. There is too much effort to be put in problem solving, instead of just getting a great sleep. We kept the Endy covering to possibly cover a future mattress for the other room, because it's washable, but we've included pictures of us about to discard the Endy mattress after receiving the Serta mattress.

Do yourself a favour and get a real hotel type mattress and start to sleep well again.
