Calii Love - First Canadian Place Review

First Canadian Place, Toronto, ON · Etobicoke

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Had the “Calii Box Signature Noodle Blend of zucchini, sweet potato glass, and kelp" from Calii Love for lunch and it was delicious. They had the blend of flavours down perfectly. It was a $21.00 salad that was delivered to our door, and they forgot to include the Yellowfish tuna. However, the straight goods is that the salad was totally worth it.
