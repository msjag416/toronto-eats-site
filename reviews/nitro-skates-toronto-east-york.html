Nitro Skates Toronto Review

852 Eglinton Ave E Second Floor, East York, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

David at Nitro Skates is excellent at what he does. He specializes in quad skates (roller skates) and helped me find the best wheels for indoor roller skating, spinning, and dancing. He had all the tools and know-how to install the new wheels, adjust my trucks, and remove the toe stopper on my Riedell boots. He also helped my girlfriend get a brand new skate setup that she was happy with and adjusted them for her as well.

Pro tip: His shop is the left door above a pizza place, competing with a sign for an esthetics service.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
