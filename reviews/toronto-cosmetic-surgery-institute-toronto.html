Toronto Cosmetic Surgery Institute Review

100 Front St W, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

If you’re considering a mommy makeover, Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL), tummy tuck, or breast augmentation, the Toronto Cosmetic Surgery Institute should be on your radar. With over 10,000 procedures under their belt, the clinic is widely known for combining cutting-edge techniques with top-notch patient care.

Leading the charge is Dr. Martin Jugenburg (Dr. 6ix), a plastic surgeon with a global reputation. His approach is a blend of artistry and precision, consistently delivering natural-looking results. With clinics in Toronto, Dubai, Kuwait, and Miami on the horizon, he’s earned his accolades for a reason.

Adding to the team is Dr. Jacqueline Rose Makerewich, a board-certified plastic surgeon with a reputation for attention to detail. Her compassionate approach and eye for aesthetics make her a standout, especially in breast augmentations and mommy makeovers.

The team doesn’t stop impressing with just their surgical skills. From the OR nurse who keep things calm to the expert anesthesiologists ensuring a smooth ride, every step of the way is handled with care. And as a recovery nurse, I help ensure patients wake up with as little pain and nausea as possible. Patients are consistently thrilled with their results, and that’s what makes the job so rewarding.

The clinic’s location within the Fairmont Royal York is just the cherry on top. Patients can continue their recovery in the comfort of a luxury hotel room, just an elevator ride away.

In short? If you’re looking for world-class results with luxury care, this is the place to be.
