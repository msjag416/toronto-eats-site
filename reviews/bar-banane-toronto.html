Bar Banane Review

227A Ossington Ave, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Sip and Savor at Bar Banane Cocktail Bar - Crafty Cocktails and Tasty Bites Await

Visit date: December 30, 2022

Our visit to Bar Banane in Toronto was nothing short of a revelation. When we climbed the stairs and stepped into the room we got an old school speakeasy vibe mixed with a modernist twist. We were transported into a world of accessible sophistication and intrigue. The atmosphere was upbeat and the female server we had was the cherry on top, with her gracious, knowledgeable, and attentive service.

The real stars of the show at Bar Banane were the expertly crafted, and deliciously addictive cocktails and appetizers. Each cocktail was a expertly crafted, and the appetizers had us ordering and eating more and more.

If you're in the Toronto area and in need of a top-notch drinking experience, look no further than Bar Banane. Make reservations on their website first for the front lounge or the bar. Trust me, you won't be disappointed.
