Wilbur's Taco Shop Review

50 Atlantic Ave, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Massive Burritos, Delicious Tacos, and Prices That Won’t Ruin Your Appetite!

We visited Wilbur’s Taco Shop in Liberty Village today, and if you’re looking for delicious tacos, enormous burritos, and prices that won’t leave you questioning your financial decisions, this place is worth checking out!

We ordered the bulgogi, steak, and Baja fish tacos, along with the steak burritos. Everything was fresh, flavourful, and generously portioned. And those burritos? They’re HUGE! You certainly won’t leave Wilbur’s wondering where your dinner went.

My favourite was the Baja fish taco. The fish was fresh and light, with a wonderfully crispy batter that reminded me of tempura. My partner loved the bulgogi taco, and the steak taco was just as tasty. As for the 1-pound steak burritos (we took them home and weighed them), the beef was tender, juicy, and full of flavour. None of that chewy, questionable steak you occasionally encounter elsewhere, where half your meal becomes an exercise in chewing!

Another thing I liked was Wilbur’s self-serve condiment station. You can help yourself to pico de gallo, different salsas, and hot sauces, putting whatever you want into little takeout containers with plastic lids. It’s a nice setup because everyone has their own idea of what kind of flavour their food should have.

The restaurant has a warm, comfortable atmosphere, and there’s also a very nice heated, convertible outdoor patio with its own bar. It’s probably quite the gathering spot for people stopping by for food and drinks after a game or event at the nearby Exhibition grounds.

We also had the pleasure of meeting one of the owners, who introduced himself as “Star,” along with another gentleman who was equally friendly. Both took the time to chat with us, and their hospitality made an already enjoyable visit even better.

Overall, Wilbur’s gets a well-deserved 5 out of 5 stars from us. Delicious food, generous portions, fair prices, a great atmosphere, and friendly servers. Really, what more could you ask for in Liberty Village? Oh yeah, it’s literally a couple of blocks away from where we live!
