NAAN KABOB Review

62 Laird Dr, East York, ON · Leaside

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Naan & Kabob has some delicious, flavourful food. We especially loved the taste of the lamb and beef dishes. As you can see from the photo they give you more than enough meat and you'll have no problem wolfing it down. This location's restaurant is clean with a nice warm ambiance and decor. The manager was friendly and cared about our experience. We were there on a Saturday evening and it wasn't too busy to find parking and get served.
