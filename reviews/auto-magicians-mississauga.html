Auto Magicians Review

1905 Mattawa Ave, Mississauga, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

If you live in Toronto, and don't want to drive all the way out to Missisauga use Marty from Autodent.ca.
Marty came to my condo garage, and did an excellent job removing the quarter sized dent from the hood of my BMW after a small bottle had dropped on it from a second floor veranda. It took him about an hour and forty-five minutes. The hood, and the paint are as good as new, and I didn't have to drive anywhere to get the job done. Highly recommended!
