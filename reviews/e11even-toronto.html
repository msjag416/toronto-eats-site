e11even Review

15 York Street Corner of York and, Toronto, ON · Harbourfront & Union Station

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

E11even, has the best burger we've ever eaten. It also has the best Sticky Toffee Pudding we've ever eaten. I can safely say this because, we have taste tested a lot of burger places, and places that offer sticky toffee pudding. The burger is not cheap by any means, but it's worth it. We keep going back, and they keep making us happy.
