Le Moque-Tortue Review

402 Main St unit a, Shediac, NB

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We went to this restaurant for dessert on July 31st, 2023:

Bistro Le Moque Tortue in Shediac, New Brunswick has one of the best tiramisu and caramel cheesecake desserts we’ve ever had. Fresh and delicious, tastes like heaven.

More than that this place is a great place to take your kids and friends to play actual old fashioned games. Indoor or outdoor you can come to this place and have a new adventure every time.

We’ll be back when we get the urge for 10/10 dessert and to try some other dishes.

Reservations recommended.
