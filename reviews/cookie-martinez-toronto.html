Cookie Martinez Review

707 Dundas St W, Toronto, ON · Kensington Market & Chinatown

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Light, tasty, and mouth watering beef empanada's with a wonderful sauce to go with it. Each order is about $6 for two. So we have a total of 4 delivered by UberEats on a weekday. The cost for this convenience was $25. Anyhoo, I would do it again, because that particular street food was that GOOD!
