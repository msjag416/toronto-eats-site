Chuck's Roadhouse Bar & Grill Review

601 Great Northern Rd, Sault Ste. Marie, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

If you are in Sault Ste. Marie, go to this restaurant. It’s a cool retro, yet modern vibe indoors and outdoors. The Harley Davidson when you first enter is beautiful. The steaks, salads and fries are tasty too, and service is given with a smile.
