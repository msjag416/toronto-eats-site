Harbourfront Centre Review

235 Queens Quay W, Toronto, ON · Harbourfront & Union Station

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

A review by W4W Toronto

www. w4wtoronto.wordpress.com

Best spot for being by the water, and having some drinks. We went there in June and had a fabulous pear, blue cheese and arugula salad. As well I had a very tasty Lychee martini. Went back recently in August and the salad was very blasé, plus the Lychee martini had no lychee juice, just SoHo and vodka...yuck. Consistency is key to a customer returning, and referring others, and Lusso is doing poorly in this regard. Stick to a beer or mixed drink, and just watch the view from the water's edge. Very gay friendly as well so that is why it scores 4 out of 5 instead of 3.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
