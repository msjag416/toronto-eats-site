Equinox Yorkville Review

55 Avenue Rd, Toronto, ON

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

I was a great place to work out, because it felt more like a spa. However, it is in transition from being The Yorkville Club into the Equinox at Yorkville. I'm no longer a member, because the transition has been so painfully slow, and the gym now looks like any other gym that has seen better days. I'm working out at Hardcandy Fitness (Madonna's gym) on Yonge. It is massive, beautiful, bright, brand new equipment, and feels less like a gym, and more like your own big play room. This place is so big, that it is never too busy, even at peak hours. The only down side is there is no validated parking in the attached underground parking lot. If the still to be completed Equinox in Yorkville is anything like the other Equinox's in North America, I'll most definitely be back (because of the free underground parking).

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
