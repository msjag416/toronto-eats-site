Organic Boutique Review

970 Queen St W, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

I’ve I’ve been going here for my cocktail bitters and aromatic for a few years. Steve, the owner is a cool and personable guy who really knows about the products he is selling. He does not sell online, so you have to make the trip to his store.

Incidentally, there is a store right beside him who sells the same type of products. That guy is not as friendly and sells his products online. Sometimes people confuse the two stores and try to return stuff they bought online to the Organic Boutique and Steve has to gently but firmly tell them that the items are not from his store. Some ignorant people don’t take kindly to this until they are shown the other rip-off store beside Organic Boutique.

I wonder how many of these reviews are actually about Organic Boutique and the owner, and not about the douche bag who runs the place just to the west of him.

Go to Organic Boutique if you have a love for the art of making the perfect cocktail. You’ll meet an owner of a store who is just as passionate about it as you.
