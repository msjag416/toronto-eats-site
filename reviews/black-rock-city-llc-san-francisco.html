Black Rock City LLC Review

660 Alabama St, San Francisco, CA, United States

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Radical self expression in a hedonistic type environment that is purposely out in the desert. Whatever is ailing you... you'll perhaps find a cure here, or at least another with your disorder. Drugs, sex, music, and creative expression are the order of the day. Go and be liberal if the above turns you on, but stay far away if you've already resolved your psychological issues with therapy. You won't need to be a part of something so bizarre and anti-social that you crave for it again as soon the embers are out on the Burning Man, or in the opposite realm... be thankful that it's over! !!! Filed under "Weirdos" and "Weirdness".

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
