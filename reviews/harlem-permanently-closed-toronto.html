Harlem - PERMANENTLY CLOSED Review

745 Queen St W, Toronto, ON

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Updated Nov 2017:

Fast track to the bottom if you want to read why I'm only giving Harlem a 2 star review.

They have THE best fried chicken you will get at a Toronto restaurant period. Their stewed chicken is absolutely delicious too. Get it with the cornbread and bacon mac n' cheese and the rice and peas.

Word of warning: if you get it delivered from UberEats or another service, the food arrives all sauced up in flimsy cardboard boxes with juices dripping everywhere. It has happened 50% of the time we ordered. I even called to ask them to change the containers back to the styrofoam ones, and an arrogant dude said "that's all we have".

So much for caring about their reputation.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
