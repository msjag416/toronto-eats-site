Bloomex Toronto Flowers & Gift Baskets Review

902 Magnetic Dr, North York, ON · North York

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

I ordered tulips from Bloomex for Mother's Day for my girlfriend on May 2016. In the picture online there were ten healthy, and full bulbed tulips.
Seven flowers were delivered on May 8th and they literally looked like they were dying. Some of the open bulbs had rotting petals, others were too small to ever open, and several of the leaves were broken and just hanging on.
I immediately called Bloomex on Sunday and described the problem to them and requested a refund. I sent pictures of the deplorable flowers. They offered to send out more flowers within 48 hours. I did not receive a follow up delivery email, nor any flowers. Ever.

After much persistence and a complaint to the BBB they sent a voucher to my email. I would never order from them again. Don't waste your time or money with this rip-off service.
