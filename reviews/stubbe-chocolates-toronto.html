Stubbe Chocolates Toronto Review

653 St Clair Ave W, Toronto, ON · Lawrence Park & Yorkdale

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Tasty truffles and delivered the day before Valentines Day. What better gift idea for the chocolate loving girl in your life than a chocolate stiletto filled with chocolate truffles? 4/5 stars because the website had shown a pair of stiletto's, but what was delivered was one shoe. An upsell would have been very appropriate in helping me decide if I wanted a PAIR of chocolate heels (as I thought I was buying) instead of just one.
