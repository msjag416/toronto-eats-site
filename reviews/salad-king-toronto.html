Salad King Review

340 Yonge St, Toronto, ON · Downtown Yonge & Dundas

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

First time I ever had pad Thai it was a shrimp pad Thai at Salad King. I thought it tasted great... maybe it did. That was 30 years ago. I have since been a dedicated Sukothai and Pai pad Thai lover. To my dismay and disappointment, after trying Salad King's shrimp "Phud Thai" recently, I can genuinely say it sucks! There isn't any love in the Salad King's version. There is barely any shrimp, or any flavour. This dish really is pathetic - worst of all it was delivered, not in house. So my only recourse is to tell the world not to bother with getting this crap delivered.
