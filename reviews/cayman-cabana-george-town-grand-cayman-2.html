Cayman Cabana Review

53 N Church St, George Town, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We've gone to Cayman Cabana every time we've visited Grand Cayman. Most recently, it was in May 2023.

When in Georgetown, Grand Cayman Island you have to stop by Cayman Cabana and have lunch or dinner. That's easy enought when you get off a cruise ship, because the restaurant is right there. They make tasty food! Everything from their fish tacos, to the seafood rundown and conch stew that you see here. Their open deck, and friendly bar service is also wonderful. Luigi is the owner, and is always hospitable.

At around 1900hrs nightly, you'll also be able to witness the Tarpon feeding off the deck of Cayman Cabana.
