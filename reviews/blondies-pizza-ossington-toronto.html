Blondies Pizza Ossington Review

88 Ossington Ave Unit 2, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Ordered from Blondie’s several times using the Ubereats app in the last couple of years and can’t believe I’ve never left a review on Google reviews. The pizza smells, looks, and tastes so good we just dig in, so I’m surprised I found a photo.

Blondie’s pizza is everything you would expect in a gourmet pizza, and they’ve always remained consistent.

Highly recommended.
