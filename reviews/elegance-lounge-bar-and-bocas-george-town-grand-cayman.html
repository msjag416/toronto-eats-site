Elegance Lounge - Bar & Bocas Review

27 Mary St, George Town, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Went on Friday, September 4, 2026.

This is a local spot in George Town, Grand Cayman. As such, we were expecting more of a modern vibe, or at least a 2026 nightclub aesthetic. No such thing.

The third floor is dark and doesn't offer much of a real club atmosphere. If you want to have a quick beer and hang with the locals in near darkness, like you're at somebody's makeshift rooftop party, sure. Technically, the third floor is called the Rooftop Lounge and is now associated with Lush Sky Lounge and Club Elegance.

We didn't go inside the venue on the second floor, officially called Elegance Lounge, because they were charging over 20 KYD to get in, and you couldn't even peek inside to see what you would be paying for. We heard the sound system, though, and from the outside, it sounded reasonable. The second-floor setup appears to function as an adult-entertainment style venue.

As two Caribbean women who have been to some of the best parties in some of the best nightclubs Grand Cayman used to have, we've seen the nightlife go downhill over the years. We didn't expect much, and that's exactly what we got.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
