sukhoTHAI Review

274 Parliament St, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Made with a green curry Sukhothai's shrimp Pad Thai is to die for. I dare you to find a better and more delicious tasting Pad Thai in Toronto. Large portion at a very reasonable price, you can't go wrong. Call ahead and take out, or go after the lunch rush for a seat in their small, quaint restaurant. Only negative is that the fried up, greasy spring rolls are awful (Dazzling Restaurant on King St W. make the best light, crispy fried spring rolls in Toronto)
