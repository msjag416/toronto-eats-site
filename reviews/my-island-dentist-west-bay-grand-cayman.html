My Island Dentist Review

Governor's Square, West Bay, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Olive had an excellent experience at My Island Dentist in Grand Cayman on Friday, August 21, 2026.

She went in because the crown on her dental implant had become loose. Dr. Talia Davidson's office contacted the Toronto dentist who originally placed the implant and obtained the information needed to identify the correct implant system.

Dr. Davidson took an X-ray, removed the loose crown painlessly, and re-cemented it properly. She then took another X-ray to confirm that everything was in place. The entire procedure was straightforward, and Olive had no pain during or after the treatment.

Dr. Talia Davidson was professional, skilled, and reassuring. Her dental assistant, Nishi, was also excellent and very attentive. We give My Island Dentist five stars out of five and highly recommend Dr. Davidson and her friendly team.

They also have a WhatsApp number, which was one of the deciding factors on why we chose My Island Dentist.
