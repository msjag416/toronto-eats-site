Salus Fresh Foods Review

777 Bay St., Toronto, ON · Downtown Yonge & Dundas

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

This place is ridiculous! We ordered "grilled" chicken, mushrooms, and "grilled" shrimp in one salad; and tofu, crunchy chick peas, and edamame in another. The salads came mixed up with a little of each in both orders. There was not grilled anything. Just chicken with an implied grilling strip on it, and the shrimp was cocktail shrimp that you would defrost and have with the red sauce.

And for the pleasure of this debacle we paid $34.95 to have it delivered to our house. Talk about first world problems, right? But a problem nevertheless, because for the expense you can't even get what you asked for.
