Allora Cayman Review

Harbour Walk, Grand Harbour, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Visited August 25, 2026.

We stopped at Allora Café for coffee and dessert and absolutely loved it. I had a cappuccino, which was stellar, smooth, rich, beautifully made, and topped with perfect foam and latte art. My girlfriend had an Americano, and her coffee was just as good. You can tell they take their coffee seriously here.

The carrot cake, though, stole the show. It was soft, moist, full of flavor, and topped with a creamy frosting that complemented the cake perfectly without overpowering it. The spices, sweetness, texture, and frosting all worked together beautifully. Even the presentation was on point.

Allora itself has a beautiful set up, with an impressive selection of cakes, pastries, gelato, and other desserts displayed inside. We chose to sit outside in the lovely courtyard, which has a relaxed atmosphere and games you can play while you're there, including cornhole and foosball.

We'll definitely be back to Allora Cayman.
