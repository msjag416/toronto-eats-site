MGM Grand Buffet Review

3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV, United States

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

No crab legs at the Sunday brunch. As well, I only really liked one thing and when it was eaten out by the masses, I had to wait forever for the kitchen to fill it up.

Overall it was a good selection of pleasant looking food with several kinds of meats, and some different types of seafood. I don't know if it was worth $35USD, because I can't eat that amount of food in one sitting. I did see several people who went up about four times in one hour though. Plates piled high to the sky. Didn't even miss out on dessert. These people feel that they are getting their money's worth, when all they are really doing is adding to their back fat, and clogging up their coronary arteries.
