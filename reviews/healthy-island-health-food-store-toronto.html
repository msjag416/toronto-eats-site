Healthy Island Health Food Store Review

5 Bastion St, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

The people who work here are Asian (Chinese most likely). Nice sitters of the cash register, but seriously under qualified to be working in a health food store. Unless you know exactly what you are going in to look for - how to spell it - and what shelf it would most likely be found on, don't bother frustrating yourself in here. The names of things like Glutathione and Selenium are mystifying to them. Even finding Tea Tree Oil was a merry (not so much) adventure. The language barrier doesn't do much to help things either. Prices are also higher than Kensington Market, but then you're paying for the convenience of the store being located at Bastion and Fleet Street.
