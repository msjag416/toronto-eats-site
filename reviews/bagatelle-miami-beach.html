Bagatelle Miami Beach Review

1669 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL, United States

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

Bagatelle
Went on a Tuesday night in Sept 2016 for the Miami Spice meal that was recommended by the concierge at Shore Club. The atmosphere was upbeat with the staff dressed in 80"s clothes and hair, and the DJ playing 80's music. The DJ would periodically turn up the music to a high decibel level and scare the crap out of some patrons, while the staff clapped and sang. We had the hanger steak and it tasted great. However, we were overcharged on our bill, and although it was corrected, our meal ended up being three times as much as we anticipated (with things like $10 for Evian water poured from bottles that are already opened and the included 18% service charge)
