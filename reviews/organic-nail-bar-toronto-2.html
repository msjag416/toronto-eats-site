Organic Nail Bar Review

35 Bathurst St, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

I've been going to this location to see Emily for almost two years now. I can't say enough great things about the place. Emily does a great job with the nail "dipping" over my own nails. My nails stay in excellent shape, and always look beautiful when first done until I return for a new colour up to 5 weeks later! Ask about the ombre and chrome over the dipping for some nice added effects that shellac simply can't match.
