Haber's Compounding Pharmacy Review

1783 Avenue Rd, Toronto, ON · Lawrence Park & Yorkdale

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Harber's is a neighborhood pharmacy in a Jewish section of Toronto. As such they overcharge you for the privilege of walking into their run down store and buying pills, elixirs, and medication that you would be better off getting at Shopper's Drug Mart or even Costco. I have been in there on numerous occasions unfortunately because they are convenient. The staff know nothing about medications and will diagnose you (unsolicited) on the spot saying you have an "infection and you need antibiotics". The Pharmacist and owner, "Marvin Malamed", also is not very conscious in the information he gives. He heard my symptoms and my desperation to relieve my condition, then he offered a "ify' cure that would take "weeks" to work. Like hello?!, is anybody home in that ancient, wreck of a place? They honestly need to get with the times and give good, knowledgeable, affordable, and consistent customer service, or get the hell out of business! IDIOTS!
