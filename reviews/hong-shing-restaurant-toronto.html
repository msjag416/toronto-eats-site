Hong Shing Restaurant Review

195 Dundas St W, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Reviewed on Feb 13, 2024

Hong Shing has been my go-to spot for the best Chinese food in Toronto for over 25 years, and it’s all about their Chinese styled lobsters. Their “Spicy Dry Lobster” isn’t just good; it’s the kind of dish you dream about. It’s packed with flavor without being too hot to handle.

Thanks to Ubereats, enjoying Hong Shing’s delights has become even easier. No more battling for parking on Dundas West just to get my fix.

And it’s not just the lobster. Their Cantonese Fried Noodle and even the mixed veggies are top-notch. Trust me, even your kids will be asking for seconds on the greens. The chefs at Hong Shing? They’ve got some serious skills.

If you’re thinking of dining in, Hong Shing is a casual spot, perfect for when you’re craving authentic Chinese food without the fuss.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
