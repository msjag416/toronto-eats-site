Dixie Uniforms Review

44 Gerrard St W G2, Toronto, ON · Downtown Yonge & Dundas

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We went to Dixie Uniforms on Gerrard St. (between Bay and Yonge) on Nov 29th 2022. You can park underground at the shops on Aura. Follow the blue "P" sign.

Teresa was very helpful in finding our sizes and styles. She had spent time asking us what we were looking for in a uniform, then was able to get them from the large selection of stock wrapped in cellophane. We were shown the latest Mobb nursing uniforms, and tried them on in the two change rooms that were available.

We didn't have too much difficulty choosing what we wanted, and used our RNAO membership to save 15% off the final price of several uniforms each.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
