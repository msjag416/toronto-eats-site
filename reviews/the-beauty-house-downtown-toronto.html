The Beauty House Downtown Review

110 Fort York Blvd, Toronto, ON

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Had microblading done here 18 months ago. It was an ok procedure at first. Then you have to go back for a touch up about 6 weeks later. That second microblading session was extremely painful. Painful like a scalpel going over a 6 week old cut over and over again! And for all that torture, do you think my brows are still on fleek? Absolutely NOT,! They have faded to the point where I'm powdering them again. $450 wasted!

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
