Heavenly Suites St. Lucia Review

Monchy Rd., La Feuillet, St. Lucia

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

Heavenly Suites is in an ideal location with mountainous views. You will definitely need a car to stay here. Having the pool is great, and the beaches are less than a 10 minute drive away. Rodney Bay is also 10 minutes away. Going during off-season (May - to the end of November) will save you money per night, but you'll also run the risk of being caught up in hurricane weather like we did for the first 36 hours of our trip.

TIP: Unless you've driven in St. Lucia before, get someone to pick you up and rent a car from in town. We rented ours from the airport. Our rental car was a 2017 Suzuki Ciaz and cost $800 USD for 14 days. Yes, ridiculously pricey, but you'll need a reliable car and you'll be offered older rentals for the same price, so do your homework ahead of time. As a first time tourist, driving from the main airport to Heavenly Suites is not recommended!

TIP: The road to Heavenly Suites is unnamed (and as of this writing so is the place). There will be "Ginette's"restaurant on the left side, and after that make a quick right at the "Teletubbies" painting on the primary school wall. We called it Teletubbies road. Ask the host for the exact description of the gates (because that's all you'll see from the road).

TIP: Get a great personal tour guide from the tour company in Rodney Bay. We found this out ourselves. Drive into Rodney Bay past the RBC, on your right side just beside "Royal Palm" restaurant . I forgot the name of the tour company, but a Rasta guy greeted us and hooked us up with our own personal tour guide for the whole day (12 hours) for $120 USD. That's $60 a person. This young man was professional, drove us in his company vehicle, and a free lunch was included. He climbed the Gros Piton with us and took us to Sugar Beach, as well as other places.
