RaviSoups Review

1128 Queen St W, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Ravi's chicken hot pot soup does not say that it is hot and spicy, but it is. I ate that along with the lamb wrap which is is also very spicy, and chewy. I don't like spicy without flavor, so I'm going to have to say that Ravi soups is not my thing, the wrap is a giant NO. I blended the soup up in my blender to get the basil to mix in there, and didn't finish the lamb wrap, so no pictures.
