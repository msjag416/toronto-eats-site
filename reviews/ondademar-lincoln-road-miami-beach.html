OndadeMar Lincoln Road Review

438 Lincoln Rd, Miami Beach, FL, United States

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

The best bikini shop in Miami Beach. Spend your money on fashionable and quality bikini wear that will last. Got a few from the Lincoln Road location and they still look great and hold up nicely after being worn and washed many times. Onda de Mar please bring your brand to Toronto, Canada and put it on Queen Street West - you won't regret it!
