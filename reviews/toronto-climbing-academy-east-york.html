Toronto Climbing Academy Review

11 Curity Ave Unit 3, East York, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

This was a great place to take my 8 and 10 year old nephew to learn rock climbing. We booked a same day drop-in appointment during March Break 2018 and spent about 21/2 hours climbing several different walls, each more challenging than the other. All equipment is provided. The mats are plush so falling onto them are not a problem. Bring your own lock for the lockers and save $2.00. Bring your own drinks too if you're into the healthy stuff and/or snacks. Parking is good and free on the premise. Total cost was less than $150 for the three of us.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
