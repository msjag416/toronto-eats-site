Thor Espresso Bar Review

35 Bathurst St, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

The cappuccino here is excellent from the first sip to the last. It's the perfect consistency, taste, color, and the right amount of foaminess.. Thor Espresso Bar also has a bit of room for chairs and tables to sit and have a chat or catch a Wi-Fi signal, but I always get my Cappy to go.
