Rainbow Rink Skating & Entertainment Center Review

101 Oliver St, North Tonawanda, NY, United States

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Amazing rollerskating in Buffalo. We went to Rainbow Rink on May 11th 2024 for their annual Borderline Skate Jam. The floor is wooden, slick and great for tricks. This rink is golden.

The artistry of the skates was wild. A totally different vibe from Toronto’s Scooters (which I love). Everybody’s wheels at Rainbow were 57mm or smaller with diameters of 32mm, and loose trucks.

I learned a lot about what I wanted to update my skates to, so I could be more free to do what I wanted on quad skates.

My girl and I had the best time at Rainbow jamming to a new blend of music ( I don’t what to call that music except it puts you in a trance) that’s different from the old school “Cutie Pie” and “More Bounce to the Ounce” roller vibes we are accustomed to.

A quality rink with dedicated quad skate aficionados.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
