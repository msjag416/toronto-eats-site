Bimmersport Automotive Inc Review

6780 Pacific Cir Unit #3, Mississauga, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

I've been going to Bimmersport since 2011. Charlie and his crew do a good job of listening, and doing what is needed to keep my 2006 330i BMW working in prime condition. They don't try to take your money unnecessarily, and give you a fair price on all their services. The waiting area is nice, and you get to look down on your baby while she is being serviced. If your BMW is out of warranty and you are still keeping it, these guys are for you.
