The Real Jerk Restaurant Review

842 Gerrard St E, Toronto, ON · Leslieville, Riverdale & The Beaches

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Went to the Real Jerk in October 2022 on a Wednesday:

The Real Jerk (on Gerrard) is still going after more than 30 years and changing locations. The chicken soup was delicious, and the rice and peas dish in the photo - tasty. The red peas soup, and ackee and salt fish were delicious too.

4/5 stars because they should bring back the real outdoor jerk smoker that they had in the beginning for that authentic jerk flavor. Jerk is not jerked without the real smoked flavor from a grill/pit.
