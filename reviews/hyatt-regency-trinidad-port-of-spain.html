Hyatt Regency Trinidad Review

1 Wrightson Rd, Port of Spain, Trinidad & Tobago

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

From beginning to end the attentiveness to guests is what makes staying here a brilliant experience. From the champagne flute greetings by the door persons, to the friendliness and accommodating nature of the manager, the Hyatt was a pleasure to stay at. Not to mention the first rate sushi bar, dining room, snack bar, infinity pool, fitness area, and spa being superior to many Toronto hotels that call themselves 5 star. Our room was clean and first class with feather pillows, down duvets, rain shower heads, a flat screen tv, wi-fi service, and had an awesome view of the port. For my birthday a free bottle of white wine was brought to my room. I can't say enough about this place. We were given a rate of $187/night, and then had it reduced to $167/night for the weekend by the manager. Their large party room had an event going on by RBC bank with local artist Kes the Band and we were able to get in and check out the sound in the space. Highly recommended for a large wedding reception. We will always be staying here when we visit Trinidad from now on.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
