The Keg Steakhouse + Bar - Leslie Street Review

1977 Leslie St, North York, ON · Don Mills

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

After our visit to Edwards Gardens on July 11th, 2025, we were in the mood for a patio, so we headed up to The Keg on Leslie Street. The timing couldn’t have been better.

We got there around 4 p.m., just before the rush hour crowd rolled in. The sun had shifted west and started dipping north, so we were still catching some great rays while sitting on their well appointed outdoor patio.

Great food, lots of sunlight, solid drinks, and excellent service made it a perfect end to the afternoon.
