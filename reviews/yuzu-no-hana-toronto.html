Yuzu No Hana Review

236 Adelaide St W, Toronto, ON · Financial District

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

We ordered two of the Teriyaki Chicken from Yuzu and it was brought to our door by Foodora. Tasty enough - yes. Worth $25 per tray - NO. The soup was boring as well. Good thing we used our $9.00 Foodora promo code. Seriously, just an average Japanese dish and Yuzu has no business charging so much for their takeout.

On another occasion, a while ago we had gone in and had something brought to us on fire, and that was good. Dining in should be the only option for Yuzu.
