My Pillow Outlet Store Review

5651 Innovation Blvd Suite 500, Shakopee, MN, United States

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Bought 2 off these pillows from the official my pillow dot com website and slept on them for 4 nights after initially throwing them in the dryer for 15min to fluff them out as recommended

The verdict: TERRIBLE. We were unable to maintain any type of neck support during the night. The crunchy noise was bothersome to the ear. The pillows got incredibly hot under our heads.
We can't be bothered returning them, and they have been thrown in the closet.

Bought a Coop Home Goods pillow from Amazon and after a five night test it seems to be doing the trick to support the neck in back and side sleep, make zero noise, and not be making our head hot.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
