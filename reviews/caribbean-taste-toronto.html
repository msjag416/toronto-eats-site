Caribbean Taste Review

224 Adelaide St W, Toronto, ON · Financial District

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

The jerk chicken was good. It was oven done, but the spices were just right so it replicated the the grilling in a barrel taste. However, that's all I can say about this place. We ordered their food from "JustEat' and everything but the jerk chicken was a disappointment. The oxtail was not tender enough, or flavourful enough. The festivals tasted like they had been stored near a smelly old sock, and the unripe yellow plantain was boiled and then fried, or something weird. The oxtail and jerk chicken came in containers with rice and peas, and it really was filled more with rice and less with meat and sauce. The rice and peas tasted like they had been taken from a pot cooked the day before. A disappointment overall.
