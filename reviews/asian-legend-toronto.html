Asian Legend Review

418 Dundas St W, Toronto, ON · Kensington Market & Chinatown

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

This use to be my go to place for Chinese food, because they didn't use MSG, and food tasted very good. Just recently on a home delivery order from the Dundas location, we were disappointed by the quality of the food and actually ended up becoming bloated, and having the runs the next day. The rice was soya sauced to death, and the ginger beef was overly gingered with every snow pea in the northern hemisphere thrown in. Searching for a piece of sliced beef was an exercise in patience. You'll get more food when you dine in as opposed to having it delivered from JustEat or Foodora, but maybe Asian Legend is under new ownership. Regardless, I wouldn't eat from this place again.
