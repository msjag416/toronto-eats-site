GoodLife Fitness Edmonton Rabbit Hill Road and 23 Ave Review

2430 Rabbit Hill Rd NW, Edmonton, AB

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Visited Sept 22nd 2022 from 20000-2240hrs..

This gym was a disappointment. The layout of equipment was not organized. The equipment was laid out according to brand or something, and not muscle group. Many of the brand equipment we liked to use from other Goodlife's were not there, or couldn't be located.

One of the leg extension machines was out of commission, and a hamstring machine should of been out of commission, but wasn't.

The worst thing however, was a male cleaner who came out of the women's washroom area while we were almost changing after our work out. He came out of the washroom area without speaking. There was no sign to alert us to this. And he just sort of smiled with his equipment and didn't say anything. Surprised, and taken aback, we decided to go on the massage chairs and let him finish cleaning.

The massage chairs are conveniently located outside the. men's change room so that they can get a gander at you laying down and being jiggled about. Good going Rabbit Hill Goodlife!

20 minutes later we go back in the change room. Again no sign to let us know he's there. We peeked around and he was no where to be found in the women's washroom. Great. We changed. We had just finished changing, and were leaving when this same pervert walks in again, unannounced in our change room. WTF? He had no cleaning equipment in his hands, and we don't know why he was there once again. I conjured up my yoga breathing so that I didn't have to lay hands on him, while he slinnked into the washroom area again.

We went to the front desk and stated our case of how we felt violated to a male Goodlife employee. All he could say was, "what? There was no sign?". No dummy! That 's what we said. Do you care about how vulnerable we felt? Obviously not. And why would the pervert put up a sign or verbally say he was coming in to clean if he wanted to see boobs and butt?!!

This gym is not worth the aggravation even if employee retraining happens. Adult females should not go here (especially late at night) unless you want strange men entering the women's change room unannounced. It's a violation waiting to happen, or an ass kickiing in the making.

We are never going back to this Goodlife.
