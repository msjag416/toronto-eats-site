Garden Gangsters Review

12 Market St., Toronto, ON · St. Lawrence Market

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Delicious salad boxes. You can't really go wrong with quinoa and other salad ingredients, and a good dressing. The only thing I didn't like was the price. Just way too expensive for my salad with chicken added. However, this place beats Freshii in every way.
