Cadillac Jenkins Review

930 Queen St W, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Ordered their jerk chicken, coleslaw, and rice and peas through Foodora. The jerk chicken was ok, the coleslaw didn't have enough sweet or saltiness, and the rice and peas was like a stew! Just look at the pictures I posted, rice and peas wet and yucky.. Not only that, the coleslaw as only enough for one person, and the rice and peas nonsense wasn't enough for even a child. Save your money, don't order from Cadillac Jenkins!
