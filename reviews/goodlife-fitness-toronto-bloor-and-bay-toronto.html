GoodLife Fitness Toronto Bloor and Bay Review

80 Bloor St W, Toronto, ON

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

I had last visited this gym 20 years ago (I think) when it was changing from Bally's to something else. At that time the two levels had great equipment with a clean feel to the gym. So I was very surprised when I moved back into the area and went to work out there. This Goodlife has some of the oldest equipment around, and the gym in general feels subdued and tired. It's weird that it would be so close to the upscale Yorkville area with the Equinox club nearby, yet have such a inferior feel. You can still get a good workout of course. However, compared to the some of other downtown Toronto locations, this Goodlife missed the opportunity to be something extraordinary.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
