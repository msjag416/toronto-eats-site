Sporting Life Review

2665 Yonge St, Toronto, ON · Midtown: Yonge & Eglinton

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

You can get a whole bunch of sporting stuff at this Sporting Life, but this location is special. It's the only place I know of to get a gorgeous Goldbergh, Parajumpers, even Bogner or Moncler winter coat/jacket on sale in Toronto. Just go in the first couple of weeks in January. They don't generally advertise "sale" prices on any of these items. You just have to go in and see the items marked down sometime by half price! It's like some secret or something. I've been hitting them up for the last few years in January and buying fashionable luxury jackets on sale that normally sell for well north of $800.00. I think I'm done with my collection now.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
