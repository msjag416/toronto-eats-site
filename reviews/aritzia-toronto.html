Aritzia Review

50 Bloor St W, Toronto, ON

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

A terrible selection of overpriced clothing. I was seriously shocked at the style of clothing this store contained. North American women are stylish, and like form fitting, shape complementing clothing. Most of these clothes would have one looking like a hobo going to a bonfire, or taking out the trash. Loose, unflattering, and cheap quality tops and bottoms. Winter coats made of poorly constructed materials, and framed with matted fake fur hair that falls out onto the coat. A brand name jean selection that was old, overpriced, and just plain unattractive. I had a $300 gift card, and spent it on a pair of Citizens of Humanity jeans that were $296 + tax. They had refused to give me the money for the gift card after I said I didn't like anything in there. It only took me an hour with the help of a sales lady to get these overpriced pants! My friend and I then left the store, never to go back to any Aritzia again. We went to nearby Holt Renfrew and got two pairs of trendy Zadig & Voltaire jeans and and a beautiful top for $324.00 on sale!

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
