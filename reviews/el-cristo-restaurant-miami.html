El Cristo Restaurant Review

1543 SW 8th St, Miami, FL, United States

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Sept 2016: Lunch at El Cristo in Little Havana, Miami was delicious. The Tostones Rellenos were the best we've ever tasted. They were green plantains (arranged like a cinnamon roll) and topped with juicy ropa vieja (stewed beef). The Yuca con Chicharrones - boiled cassava with fried pork chunks and special sauces. To drink, the best mango mojito you'll ever taste. This food was so tasty we little literally ate it all with just our fingers. El Cristo is an awesome Cuban food experience for a very reasonable price.
