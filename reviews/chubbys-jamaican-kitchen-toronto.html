Chubby's Jamaican Kitchen Review

104 Portland St, Toronto, ON · King West & Entertainment District

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Extremely average tasting oxtail and rice and peas. The menu said the stewed oxtail had broad beans in it. I found one. The oxtail was not seasoned with cloves, Allspice or any of the other traditional Jamaican seasonings that would have it taste as it should. The rice and peas did not have coconut milk or thyme in it. Even the vinegarized coleslaw tasted like it had been left out at room temperature all day and was going off. I don't see how anyone could get chubby off eating the crap at Chubby's. It's not even worth a picture post. I make much tastier oxtail and rice and peas and I'm a Trinidadian baby! Shame on you; you don't expect real island people to like your less than mediocre food do you?
