Museum of Illusions Toronto Review

132 Front St E, Toronto, ON · Distillery District & Corktown

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We went on a weekday on April 12th 2024.

The Museum of Illusions is a fun adventure right in downtown Toronto and is totally worth a visit. Parking can be challenging on the street, so expect to pay for indoor parking nearby.

There were just the right amount of people when we visited. You could move around and see all of the different types of illusions with ease. The museum staff were very helpful; they assisted with taking pictures to capture the best angles of the illusions and explained some of the trickier exhibits. Their explanations made it easier to understand the illusions.

Overall, we had a great time, laughing with each other and with other visitors.

We live in downtown Toronto and didn’t realize that this was an attraction to add to the list of things to do as a tourist in your own city.
