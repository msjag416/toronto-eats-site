Liberty Shawarma Review

114 Atlantic Ave, Toronto, ON · Liberty Village & Parkdale

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

This Liberty Shawarma in June 19th, 2020 was the most racist and horrendous experience I’ve ever had there. The two server people totally disregarded me as the 3 customer in their 3 customer limit store. Another white male walked in and they started taking his order and told me I was the fourth customer. The white guy excused himself and they still were not friendly. They asked for my order and simply believed the guy stepped out. I told them I was legitimately the third customer and was greeted with “what’s your order?” by the female server behind the cash. The male behind the cash was saying things like can I lower my voice. Why am I arguing? I was simply trying to state facts. However, this establishment with these particular people on this day was giving me the racist vibe. They treated me like they were doing me a favour. Instead of me doing them a favour and spending money at their place. I persisted with my order, but they had the nerve to ask for a tip which I didn’t give. The whole episode should have been embarrassing to them, because it showed they are prejudice toward people of colour and have us ALL judged as people who apparently don’t follow the rules. As I was leaving I heard the male server say f*ck off and get the f*ck out of here when he thought I couldn’t hear. So after going here for years and spending at least a thousand dollars on food, these two strangers try to make me feel like I am not a worthy customer. Well they didn’t succeed. This posting will reflect how feel about this place.
