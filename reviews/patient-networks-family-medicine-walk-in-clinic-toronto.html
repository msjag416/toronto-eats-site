Patient Networks Family Medicine Walk In Clinic Review

157 Yonge St, Toronto, ON · Financial District

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

A very good walk-in clinic with excellent doctor service. I especially liked dealing with Dr. Sheinerman The reason I gave it 4 stars, is because during the COVID-19 lockdown the receptionists would not answer the telephone during working hours. You were left to rely on email which was neither quick or efficient.
