Joe's New York Style Pizza Review

345 Amherst St, Buffalo, NY, United States

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Authentic New York-style pizzas can be found at Joe’s New York Style Pizza. We got our slices on May 11th, 2024.

The pizza is thin, the crust is crispy, and the slices are those classic large triangles. The best for us was the cheese pizza. We don’t usually crave a plain cheese on tomato sauce pizza in Toronto, but Joe’s changed our minds. It’s never smelled or tasted this good. When you go to New York, a thin crust cheese slice is a must, and Joe’s does it right.

There are other pizza slices to tempt you in the glass enclosure, and you should try them to compare, but the cheese slice wins every time. Oh, and you’ve got to eat it folded — like a real New Yorker.

Joe’s restaurant isn’t fancy, but it’s clean. The service is good, the price is reasonable, and they also deliver to the surrounding area. But if you’re like us, you’ll cross the Canadian border if you’re in the Niagara region to get it.

Highly recommend.
