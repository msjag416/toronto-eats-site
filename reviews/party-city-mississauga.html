Party City Review

Power Centre, Mississauga, ON

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

I went to Party City at this 3050 Vega Blvd yesterday and was sorely disappointed.

Several disappointments included:

- the store was depressing (dim lighting, non festive atmosphere)
- low supply and quality of Christmas wrapping paper. What was left was pathetic.
- low supply and quality of decent bows. What was left was ridiculous.
- unhelpful staff directing me to places to look for things that didn't exist then saying "oh I guess we're all out"
- Not wanting to leave empty-handed, I bought 2 shiny picked over green rolls of wrapping paper and a very large Christmas gift bag. The sales girl tried to put them into tiny plastic bags to give me so that they could fall out on the the snow outside. I asked for a bigger bag and she said "oh we're all out" without a care in the world.

The verdict: I hate this ugly looking place and won't be back. Also, don't go here unless you want to be depressed.
