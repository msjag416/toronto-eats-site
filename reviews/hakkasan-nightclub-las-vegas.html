Hakkasan Nightclub Review

3799 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV, United States

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

The best part of the MGM Grand hotel is the nightclub Hakkasan. It is literally right in the hotel. It's a walk to meander through the main MGM building, but you've got to go to this beautiful multilevel complex!!! Tiesto was playing techno house on the top floor, and the R&B was bumping on the lower levels. The place was packed, the people were friendly and the whole vibe was about having a good time.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
