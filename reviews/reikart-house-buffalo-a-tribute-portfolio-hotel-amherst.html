Reikart House, Buffalo, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel Review

5000 Main St, Amherst, NY, United States

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We stayed at Reikart House, Buffalo, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel, using my many thousands of Marriott points.

Parking was ample and free—no need to squeeze into a tight spot like a sardine. Reception was easy to find, and the receptionist was as friendly as your favorite barista. The hotel is very clean, cozy, and quaint, like stepping into a charming storybook.

Our room was on the second floor and had a fireplace—a nice touch. The king bed was comfy enough to make you forget you aren’t royalty. The Keurig coffee maker worked perfectly, ensuring no caffeine-deprived grumpiness. Bottled water was free, and the mini fridge was just right for our beverages.

We really liked the little touches, such as a full-length mirror for admiring your travel outfits, makeup lighting for flawless selfies, and a black makeup towel to avoid those dreaded makeup stains. The bathroom was big enough for two people to be in at the same time without turning it into a splash zone.

The large flat-screen TV was clear and perfectly positioned above the fireplace, with what seemed like hundreds of channels—ideal for channel surfing.

Room service for breakfast was easy and tasty the next morning—just the way to start Mother’s Day for my girlfriend.

Check-out was a breeze. They even had my credit card ready at the desk for me after the manager at the Jazzboline restaurant ran it over (I had forgotten it the night before, too busy enjoying dinner).

Overall, a very good experience and highly recommend it as a hotel to stay at in the Niagara Falls area on the Buffalo side.
