Geary Avenue Warehouse Review

209 Geary Ave, Toronto, ON

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We went to Futura Sound Presents: ART OF US with Miss Tara on Friday, October 10th at the Geary Street warehouse, and the atmosphere was EDM electric.

The turnout filled the space, but it never felt too crowded. The room layout, lighting, and ceiling lasers gave it a creative underground feel while still being comfortable to move around and dance.

The sound system was powerful and crisp, with deep bass that carried through the whole warehouse. The light show and visuals created a perfect balance of energy and ambience.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
