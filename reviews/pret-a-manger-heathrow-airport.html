Pret A Manger Review

Heathrow Airport, United Kingdom

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

I wish a Pret A Manger would open up in Toronto, or at least somewhere in North America. This place puts Tim Hortons, and Subway to shame. If you want a tasty to-go sandwich or healthy, but tasty salad, this is a franchise that does it right.

August 2015

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
