Cold Tea Review

1186 Queen St W, Toronto, ON

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Went with some friends Sunday May 29th 2016. Great eclectic vibe, friendly people, great music and sound system outside (R&B, rap, hip-hop, dancehall reggae), nice moderate sized patio, outdoor cook-out, nice cocktails, smokers go on the side on the patio to smoke. Now for some things to be aware of: location may be a bit tricky to find even when you enter Kensington Mall, they take CASH ONLY (but there is an ATM that charges a $2.00 surcharge), the line-up for drinks can be a pain with only two bartenders (drinks are not that cheap), after 4pm when we were leaving we saw a MASSIVE line up to get in, With those things in mind, go in prepared, and go to have fun!
