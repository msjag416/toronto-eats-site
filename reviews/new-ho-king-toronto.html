New Ho King Review

410 Spadina Ave., Toronto, ON

★★☆☆☆ 2/5 Not Recommended

Uber Eats Order - May 18, 2024

I’ll break down our recent Uber Eats delivery from New Ho King.

Pork Fried Rice: Not bad at all. It had a good balance of flavors, but nothing to write home about.

Sliced Chicken in Sauce: This was just okay. Standard fare, nothing memorable.

Ginger and Scallion Lobster: Fair at best. I had higher hopes, but it was pretty underwhelming.

Overall: This place is overrated, riding high on Kendrick Lamar's mention. There are many better Chinese restaurants in downtown Toronto. Hong Shing is still our go-to favorite. Save your money and your taste buds.
