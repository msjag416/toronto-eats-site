Courtyard by Marriott Calgary South Review

3750 Market St, Calgary, AB

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

We stayed at the Courtyard by Marriott Calgary South from Sept 19th to 21st, 2022

We had an enjoyable experience for the time we were there.

The rooms:
Modern, clean, and tastefully appointed. Comfortable king-sized bed.

The staff:
Helpful service with a smile.

The food:
The food in the lobby at Rendezvous was great. They have a turkey club sandwich that’s delicious, and addictive. Their pasta’s for dinner were tasty. Their chicken wings were crispy on the outside and moist on the inside and delicious. The Caesar salad was very nice. Their alcoholic drinks were made properly, and the old-fashioned is presented “smoking” literally.

We highly recommend this hotel when in Calgary, and it’s only a 20 minute drive to downtown.
