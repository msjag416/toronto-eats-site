Bar Poet Review

1090 Queen St W, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Ordered via Ubereats from Bar Poet on June 16, 2023

This review is just about two of Bar Poet’s pizzas.

In a nutshell, the thin crust pizzas that arrived at our door looked nothing like the pizzas on the Uber app.

I ordered a ”Shrimp Alfredo” pizza. My 12” pizza arrived with 6 slices and 7 helpless shrimp strewn about the box and absent of any Alfredo looking sauce or moisture. I bit into a slice and it was utterly boring. I had to gussy up my pizza with sprinkling a can of tuna over it, some spices, and adding some goat cheese and shoving it in the air fryer. I would call their shrimp pizza a TOTAL RIPOFF.

The other 12” thin crust pizza that was delivered was the “Casablanca” and was only decent because of the banana peppers, otherwise not worth ordering or mentioning.

Bar Poet does not do pizza’s well. Period. Order a Blondie’s pizza and you’ll see what a real gourmet pizza looks, and tastes like when it arrives.
