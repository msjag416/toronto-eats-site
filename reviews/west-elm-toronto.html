west elm Review

109 Atlantic Ave, Toronto, ON · Liberty Village & Parkdale

★★★☆☆ 3/5 Mixed

A pretty good selection of some hard to find pieces for the condo crowd. However, selling fluffy throw pillows for $110.00 + tax is ridiculous in Liberty Village. You can't even wash that type of pillow cover. Every 18 months or so we have gone to replace these pillows and the price is now double what they were originally.

Our other pet peeve is the terrible selection of drinking glasses. Just plain square glasses of a long tubular nature, and made thick enough for the dishwasher would be ideal. What is so hard about supplying that?!
