Lahore Tikka House Review

1365 Gerrard St E, Toronto, ON · Leslieville, Riverdale & The Beaches

★★★★☆ 4/5 Recommended

Spicy, flavorful, authentic (North?) Indian meals. Big portions with low key styling. So low key in fact, you use styrofoam plates and cups, and leave smelling like curry and such. But hey it's worth it.

Only complaint is that the Butter Chicken has like only 4 pieces of chicken in a saucy container that could feed 8 people. As a matter of fact most of the meat dishes hardly have enough meat for the price, but taste so damn good! Come on stop being so cheap with the meat! The rice dishes are plentiful and so is the naan bread. In terms of portions AND flavor, the Host Restaurant has them beat (but they are more expensive).
