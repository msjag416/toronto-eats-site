Barque Smokehouse Review

299 Roncesvalles Ave, Toronto, ON · The Junction, High Park & Roncesvalles

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Ordered the Barque Smokehouse barbeque food online and it is terrible. The meat is NOT tender and flavorful, and is covered in a smoky tasting sauce that you can buy off the self. The Barque Smokehouse fried chicken looks the same colour as the brisket, but is over battered with crap, AND still tastes like you might as well be eating a rubber glove. The shoe-string fries are overdone and tastes weirdly of "cinnamon". Barque Smokehouse even has an arugula salad that has only arugula, caramel popcorn, and an unidentified vegetable in it! Please put a fork in this restaurant and consider it done.

Reviewed by MsJAG, Level 7 Google Local Guide. Self-funded visit, no sponsorship.
