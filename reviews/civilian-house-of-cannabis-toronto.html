Civilian House Of Cannabis Review

734 Queen St W, Toronto, ON · Queen West & Trinity Bellwoods

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

I don’t smoke weed, and trust me, it’s not for lack of trying. It’s just not my thing. But if it were, and I was on the hunt for the crème de la crème of exotic strains or edibles, there’s no question where I’d go. Civilian on Queen St West isn’t just a dispensary; it’s a sensory haven, effortlessly blending aesthetics and ambiance. In a word: stunning.

Now, here’s the hidden gem I’m sharing: if you’re into Amapiano music and crave that cozy, friends-at-home vibe, you need to check out their Friday and Saturday nights with DJ Rich in the basement. I hit up the spot on Saturday, August 3rd, 2024, and let me tell you, it was an experience.

Imagine an underground party right beneath Civilian, where the music is electric, and the atmosphere is magnetic. DJ Rich spins tracks that make you forget your inhibitions. RAFDM Underground is the place to be every Friday and Saturday. Don’t miss out on this secret hotspot—it’s everything you’ve been waiting for.
