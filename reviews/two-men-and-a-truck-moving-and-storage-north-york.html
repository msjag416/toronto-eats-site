Two Men and a Truck Moving and Storage Review

161 Bartley Dr #12, North York, ON · Don Mills

★☆☆☆☆ 1/5 Avoid

Used Two Men and a Truck several years ago to move. We were quoted a fair price and two men. Three men arrived, and the price increased. They said "hey take it or leave it". The move to the unit was fine, but one guy stayed with the truck, and the other two argued the whole time while moving items together. One was a coke head with a "bladder problem" that used our washroom many times during the move. They also failed to wrap a mirror properly, and broke it. We would never use them again, nor recommend Two Men and a Truck.
