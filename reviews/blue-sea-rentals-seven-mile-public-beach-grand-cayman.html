Blue Sea Rentals Review

Cabana #4, Seven Mile Public Beach, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

★★★★★ 5/5 Editor's Pick

Visited Thursday, August 27, 2026.

My girlfriend, her sister, and I spent the day on Seven Mile Beach in Grand Cayman and had a fantastic experience with Blu Sea Rentals. When we arrived, George met us right in the parking lot, we paid for our chairs and umbrellas, and he directed us down to the beach where Neville took over. Neville set us up with chairs and umbrellas right by the crystal-clear water and made sure we were comfortable from the start. The cruise ship crowd was definitely there that day, with lots of people enjoying the gorgeous beach, hot white sand, and beautiful Caribbean water, but George and Neville were able to accommodate everyone who paid for chairs and umbrellas.

These guys looked after us all day. They adjusted our umbrellas whenever the sun moved, provided free water, and even brought us ice for our cooler. Then, when we ran out of cooler space and beer, they went to the liquor store and picked up more for us. Now that is service. George and Neville were busy, helpful, friendly, and genuinely nice young men who made sure three women spending the day at the beach had everything we needed.

Paying for the chairs and umbrellas is totally worth it. That Cayman sun is hot, and so is the white sand. Being able to sit comfortably in the shade, right beside that incredible turquoise water, made it possible for us to stay and enjoy the beach for hours.

We had one of our best beach days on the island and would absolutely use Blu Sea Rentals again. If you're heading to Seven Mile Beach, look for George and Neville.
